# ADR-024: Refunds and unique bank matching

Status: Accepted. Extends ADR-003/022; the user's explicit instruction replaces manual confirmation for a sole unambiguous candidate.

Automatically link an exact account/amount match within seven days when the candidate relationship is one-to-one. Apply after bank batches, after receipt finalization and from an authorized local reconciliation action; never make provider calls or reset its lease for matching. A sole bank expense enriches the receipt's existing financial movement; multiple candidates require selection. Preserve explicit separate-purchase choices and do not delete approved/categorized transactions to guess a duplicate.

A refund is a positive cash movement linked to its original household expense. Store it as the existing income movement kind plus a nullable self-reference, preserving old-image account arithmetic; expose it separately from earned income. Net spending/category usage subtract refunds on the refund's actual date, without rewriting closed original months. Support partial refunds, cumulative caps and exact proportional allocation against the original's remaining categories. An initially unallocated purchase is categorized at its source; its refunds inherit the categories atomically. Original financial changes cannot invalidate existing refunds. Backend guards and deferred database checks protect totals, references and household boundaries.

Only a genuine booked credit can be an automatic refund. Require a stable bank reference, matching account, compatible normalized merchant, a purchase within 90 prior days, and one eligible original. Never infer refunds from two equal debits or salary matching only an amount. First-import credits may match existing recorded movements but do not silently create new financial records. Previously recorded legacy bank incomes without an explicit matching decision may be reclassified when the same unique refund criteria hold; this reuses their existing cash movement. Explicit new income decisions are never overridden. Manual refund entry and reclassification remain available. Record automatic/manual link provenance; an incorrect refund can be changed to an ordinary inflow without recording the cash twice.
